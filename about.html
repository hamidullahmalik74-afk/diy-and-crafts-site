About DIY and Crafts

Hi, I am Hamid Ullah, and I run DIY and Crafts. This site is for people who want to make something beautiful at home without spending a lot of money or needing years of experience.

I started this blog to share the projects I actually make: thrift store furniture flips, easy hand embroidery, beginner crochet, beaded accessories, and handmade gifts for Christmas and other holidays. Every project here is tested at home, with a clear supply list, numbered steps, time and cost, so you know what you are getting into before you start.

What you will find here

  • Furniture flips: thrifted dressers, tables and shelves, painted and repaired on a budget.
  • Embroidery and sewing: beginner stitches, free patterns, and simple hoop art.
  • Crochet: free patterns for bags, blankets and home items, using only basic stitches at first.
  • Quick crafts: 15 to 60 minute projects, including bag charms, candles, paper flowers and gift ideas.
  • Seasonal crafts: Christmas gifts, spring wreaths, and handmade cards.

How I write tutorials

I show the finished project first, then the supplies, then the steps in order. I include the mistakes I made, so you can avoid them. If a project needs a pattern or template, I say so up front and link to it in the post. I do not copy tutorials from other sites. All photos and instructions on this site are my own work, unless I clearly credit a source.

Most readers come from Pinterest in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia. I write in US English, use US measurements and prices in USD, and choose projects that work with supplies you can find at a craft store or thrift store.
